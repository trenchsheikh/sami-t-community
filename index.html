HOW IT HAPPENED / 003

A few chapters.

Different rooms. The same belief that people deserve a way in.

01 / YOUTH AI LITERACY

Make the invitation impossible to miss.

I led youth engagement and the organic recruitment campaign for a national AI literacy programme with Google DeepMind, the University of Cambridge and BBC R&D. The pilot selected 50 students and brought them together with 50+ Google volunteers.

~700 applications / <12 hours
02 / THE CAMDEN ALGORITHM

Bring opportunity into the neighbourhood.

On the steering committee, I helped shape an AI education event with Google and Havas that welcomed more than 240 young people into Google’s London HQ.

240+ young people
